Palmwine Merchants

The website for a Lagos palm-wine brand: book the bar for an event, order bottles and kegs, and reserve a place at the weekly Sunday Table.

Condition: customers here decide on WhatsApp, not in a checkout. So the site does the pricing up front and hands a complete request over to WhatsApp.

What it does

  • Event booking with a running estimate. Pick the kind of event, date, area and number of guests, and the estimate updates as you go: per-guest price, service charge within Lagos and 7.5% VAT. The request then goes to WhatsApp, where the final price and deposit are agreed.
  • A shop for 50 cl bottles and 5 and 25 litre kegs, with a basket that places the order on WhatsApp.
  • The Sunday Table, a weekly gathering, with its own reservations and photos from past events.
  • 18+ notices throughout, as an alcohol brand needs.

How I built it

The live site is built in Lovable, an AI website builder. I set up the structure, the booking and ordering flows and the estimate calculation, and I keep the site updated for the client. An earlier version was a Django site, still on GitHub.
