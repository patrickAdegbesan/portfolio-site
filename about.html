Background

I graduated from Caleb University in 2026 with a B.Sc. in Computer Science. My final-year project, AttendX, took class attendance using QR codes and face recognition.

I'm a software engineer at 3O1Atech, a small product studio, where I build CampusDash, ZeDI and ShelfFlow. Alongside that I take on freelance clients: an inventory and point-of-sale system and website for Fortune et Feveur, a tiling company, and websites for 3O1Atech and Palmwine Merchants. I've also entered two hackathons. I haven't worked in a large engineering team yet, and it's one of the things I'm looking for.

How I work

I use AI coding assistants every day, and my commit history shows it. They write a lot of first drafts. I decide how the system is built, set the rules it has to keep, review every change, debug what breaks and write the tests that prove it works. That's the part I'd want an employer or client to judge me on.

I write down decisions and limits as I go. Each case study on this site says which parts I built and which were shared, and what isn't finished.
