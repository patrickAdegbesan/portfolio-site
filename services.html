What I can build for you

I take on freelance projects for businesses and founders. Below is who each service suits, what you get, the work it's based on, and what isn't included. Price and timeline are agreed in writing for each project.

Tell me about your project

Business websites

For
Shops, service businesses, event brands and professionals who need a site that looks credible and loads quickly on mobile data.
You get
A new site or a redesign that works on phones first; WhatsApp, email and map links; page titles, descriptions and link previews set up for search and sharing; hosting set up on your own account; notes on how to update it.
Based on
The 3O1Atech and Palmwine Merchants websites, the Fortune et Feveur company site, and this one.
Not included
Logo and brand design, photography and copywriting. I can work from your designer's files or your existing brand.

MVP web apps

For
Founders and small teams who need a working first version to put in front of customers.
You get
Sign-in and user roles, a database, payments through Paystack, an admin panel, deployment, and automated tests on the parts that handle money.
Based on
ZeDI and CampusDash.
Not included
Licences and regulatory approval (for example, for financial products). I'll point out where your product may need them, but getting them is yours to do. Card details are handled by the payment provider, never stored in your app.

Dashboards and point of sale

For
Businesses tracking stock, sales, orders or staff in spreadsheets, or using a till that doesn't fit how they sell.
You get
Admin dashboards and reports, stock and sales systems that keep working offline, receipt printing, and apps for supported Android card terminals.
Based on
F&F Tiles POS, the CampusDash terminal apps and ShelfFlow.
Not included
Hardware. Terminal apps depend on the terminal maker's developer access and approval, which can take time and isn't in my control.

Also available

  • Mobile app MVPs with Expo, for iOS and Android from one codebase. App Store and Play Store review is decided by Apple and Google; neither of the apps I've built is in the public stores yet.
  • API integrations: Paystack, Payaza, WhatsApp, SMS and identity-check providers. You'll need your own accounts with each provider.

How a project starts

  1. You send me a short description of what you need, by email or WhatsApp.
  2. I reply with questions, then a written scope: what's included, what isn't, and how we'll know it's done.
  3. We agree the price and timeline in writing before any work starts.
  4. I build in stages and share a working preview at each one.
  5. At handover, the code and hosting are in your accounts, with notes on how to run and change them. Support after handover is agreed separately.
